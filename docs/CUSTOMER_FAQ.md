# Customer FAQ

## Booking

**How do I book an appointment?**
Go to "Book Now," pick a service, choose a staff member (or "Any Available"), pick a date and time from what's actually open, fill out a consent form if the service needs one, and review your total before paying.

**Why do I need to pay a deposit?**
It secures your slot. The deposit is a percentage of the service price (or a flat amount for some services) — you'll see the exact figure before you pay. The remaining balance is due when you check out at the salon after your service.

**Can I tip when I book?**
Yes, tipping is optional and shown at checkout with 15% / 18% / 20% preset options or a custom amount. Your tip goes to card along with your deposit and is credited straight to the staff member who did your service.

**What if no times are available on the day I want?**
Join the waitlist for that date — you'll get an email the moment a matching slot opens up (from a cancellation or schedule change), with a link to book it directly. Waitlist offers expire after 48 hours if unclaimed.

## Payment

**Is my card information safe?**
Yes — your card details go straight to Stripe (a PCI-compliant payment processor) and never touch the salon's servers directly.

**Will I be charged again automatically?**
Only in one case: a **no-show fee**, if you don't show up and don't cancel in advance (see below). Otherwise, your card is only charged the deposit + tip amount you approved at checkout, plus whatever remaining balance you pay in person.

## Cancellations & rescheduling

**What's the cancellation policy?**
Free cancellation up to 24 hours before your appointment. Cancelling within 24 hours incurs a fee (shown in the salon's cancellation policy on the booking page). If you already paid a deposit, you'll be refunded the deposit minus that fee.

**Can I reschedule instead of cancelling?**
Yes — from "My Appointments," reschedule to a new date/time for the same service and staff member, subject to the same availability rules as a new booking.

**What happens if I just don't show up?**
If you're marked as a no-show (still pending 30 minutes after your appointment start time with no check-in), a no-show fee is charged to the card on file and you'll get an emailed receipt. After 3 no-shows, your account is temporarily blocked from booking new appointments — contact the salon if you believe this was in error.

## After your appointment

**Can I leave a review?**
Yes, from "My Appointments" once a completed visit shows up under the Past tab.

**Where do I see my tip history?**
"Tips History" in your account shows every tip you've given, with the amount and which staff member received it.

## Account

**Do I need an account to book?**
Yes — this lets you manage upcoming appointments, see your history, and get reminders/confirmations by email.

**I didn't get a confirmation email.**
Check your spam folder first. Confirmation emails include a calendar invite (.ics file) you can add to Google/Apple/Outlook calendar directly. If it's genuinely missing, your appointment still shows correctly under "My Appointments" — the email is a convenience, not the source of truth for your booking.

**My account says I have booking restrictions.**
This means you've been blocked, most commonly after 3 no-shows. Contact the salon directly to resolve it.
